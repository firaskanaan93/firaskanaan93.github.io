About

Full-stack engineer, from schema to production

I'm a senior full stack engineer with 8+ years of experience delivering web applications end to end: data models and APIs, Vue.js front ends, background processing, deployment pipelines and production monitoring.

At String I build a multi-tenant marketing-automation SaaS with deep social-platform integrations. Before that, at Dar Auction, I took a real-time auction platform from inception to production, owning the Laravel API, the Socket.io bidding layer, the Vue.js website and the delivery pipeline.

Alongside hands-on engineering, I've introduced agile practices to teams, coordinated delivery across multiple teams, and trained computer engineering students in Java, mobile development and Laravel.

  • SaaS platforms

    Multi-tenant architecture with tenant isolation, RBAC, tenant-aware queues, usage metering and per-tenant billing.

  • API integrations

    Meta Graph, TikTok Business and Snapchat Marketing APIs with OAuth 2.0, token rotation, rate limits and signed webhooks.

  • Real-time systems

    Live bidding and instant updates with Socket.io, backed by a high-performance, secure Laravel API.

  • Reliability & delivery

    Idempotent jobs, retries and dead-letter queues, monitoring and alerting, Docker environments and CI/CD pipelines.
